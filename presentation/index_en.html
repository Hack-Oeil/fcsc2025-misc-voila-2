Challenge #542

Voilà

Alice loves listening to music. Unfortunately, she misconfigured her firewall and unintentionally exposed her music collection to the Internet. Alice organizes her personal collection in an unconventional way: instead of sorting by genre, album, or artist, she renames her songs based on her imagination.

Thus, she is confident that you will not be able to find the song name associated with the flag.opus file. Prove her wrong.

The flag format is: FCSC{Artist_Track}. For example FCSC{ABBA_Gimme! Gimme! Gimme! (A Man After Midnight)} . The flag is case-insensitive.

Alice's music collection is exposed on localhost:4000.

This challenge is also available in an easier version: Voilà (Baby).
